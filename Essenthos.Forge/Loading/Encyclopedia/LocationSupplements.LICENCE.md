# Geographic supplements, 26 October 2026

The OpenBible identifications and their scores retain their existing CC BY 4.0
source. Nineteen record/name bindings were read against the gazetteer's named
entries and their exact Bible citations; they do not merge records or change any
presence verdict. The original withheld OSM points remain excluded.

Three independent coordinate readings replace only the point of the same named
modern feature, without changing the identification's rank, score or alternatives:

- **Jabal Nabā / Mount Nebo:** UK Permanent Committee on Geographical Names,
  *Jordan Toponymic Factfile* (2025), page 8. The table prints 31°45′44″N,
  035°44′46″E for Jabal Nabā, aliases En Neba / Jabal an Nabā / Har Nebo.
  The sole numerical adaptation is degrees + minutes/60 + seconds/3600.
  [Exact PDF](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/694152af5431f4f94d7f0aca/Jordan_Toponymic_Factfile.pdf).
  © Crown copyright 2025. Contains public sector information licensed under the
  [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/).
  [GOV.UK reuse terms](https://www.gov.uk/help/reuse-govuk-content) apply to its
  published content; this uses the coordinate table, not logos or third-party maps.
- **Tell Abu Sefeh:** Carl Drews and Weiqing Han, *Dynamics of Wind Setdown at
  Suez and the Eastern Nile Delta*, PLOS ONE 5(8): e12481 (2010), Table 1.
  [Article and terms](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0012481), CC BY.
  The named site is explicitly listed at 30.8606 N, 32.3543 E. No hydrodynamic
  or biblical-route conclusion from the article is adopted.
- **Sarepta:** Wikidata Q616837, revision 2521611391, P625: 33.4575 N,
  35.295833333333 E. This is the Phoenician site in Lebanon, not the town in
  Louisiana. The structured data is CC0 1.0, as recorded in
  `Resources/Wikidata/LICENCE.md`; the held snapshot is `items.jsonl`.

**Wilderness of Judah / Judaean Desert:** Wikidata Q2046004, revision 2549290202,
P625 (31.7 N, 35.3 E), from that same held CC0 snapshot. This is a representative
point of the named region, with no invented border, camp location, radius or
scholarly probability. Its score is zero because this source supplies no score;
it is not assigned a percentage of scholarly agreement.

The exact downloaded PCGN/PLOS/terms bytes and their SHA-256 manifest are held
under `Resources/PlaceLocationSources/2026-10-26`. Source attributions accompany
the primary and alternative coordinate responses. Source reading does not mean
either institution endorses Essenthos or the biblical identifications.
