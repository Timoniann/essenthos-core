## What NoDerivatives reaches here (RUL-0183)

The text is served unchanged, every word and mark of punctuation as the edition prints it, and no correction is
made to it (owner, 2026-11-24). The links to the Hebrew and the Greek are a layer beside the text and change none
of its words; the owner holds that showing the text verbatim with such a layer around it is not a derivative
(TSK-1997, comment of 2026-11-23).

## Alignment

originals only through the project's own composition (KJV and BSB as middle texts), held to the hand floors of
