# *.usfm — the unrevidierte Elberfelder Bibel of 1905

**Elberfelder Bibel, unrevidierte Ausgabe**, the German the Brethren made at Elberfeld: the New
Testament in 1855 and the whole Bible in 1871, by **John Nelson Darby** (1800–1882), **Carl
Brockhaus** (1822–1899) and **Julius Anton von Poseck** (1816–1896). What is served here is the
edition of **1905**, before the revisions R. Brockhaus made from 1960 onwards.

From <https://ebible.org/deuelo/>, read at the source on **2026-09-06** and re-read on
**2026-09-20**. The archive taken is `https://ebible.org/Scriptures/deuelo_usfm.zip`;
`scripts/fetch-ebible.ps1 -Only Elberfelder1905` is what fetches it, and it re-reads every statement
below before it replaces anything.

**66 books, 31,101 verses, 721,754 words**, and **no annotation of any kind** — no Strong numbers,
no lemmas, no morphology, no alignment. It states its own verse numbering in 261 places, where
eBible has renumbered the text to the English scheme. Acts 15:34 it prints as a bare dash, which has
no words to load, which is why it holds 31,101 verses where Luther holds 31,102.

## The one statement that disagrees, and why it is not the one taken

CrossWire distributes the same edition as `gerelb1905`, marked `DistributionLicense=Public Domain`,
while its own description line in the same file reads *"Copyright by R. Bockhaus Verlag, Germany"*.
One file saying both things is not a statement anybody can rely on, which is why the reading taken
is eBible's. What R. Brockhaus does hold is the **revised** Elberfelder of 1960 and after, which is
a different text and is not this one.
