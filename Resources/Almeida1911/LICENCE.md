## Why this transcription

Clear Bible's Portuguese alignment (`ClearBible/…/JFA11`) names this eBook as its target, and its
token file reads word for word as this file does once the transcription's underscores are removed.
CrossWire's `PorAlmeida1911` is a different transcription (from manuscript4u) distributed under the
GPL; it was not taken.

## How the file is read (`AlmeidaTextSource`)

- A verse is a paragraph opening with its number; the first verse of a chapter opens with the
  chapter's number, as the printing sets it in a large initial.
- `_…_` is italics, the translator's supplied words, loaded as supplied spans.
- `[1]` marks a cross reference listed after the chapter; `[A]` an alternative rendering listed
  under NOTAS at the end. Both become verse notes (20,287 and 1,070), never words.
- Italic headings, marginal dates in brackets and parallel-passage lines are the editors' and are not
  loaded. A plain paragraph before a psalm's first verse is its title (116 of them).
- The Hebrew letters over Lamentations and Proverbs 31 and the transcribers' query `[_o_?]`
  (Numbers 5:3) are not words of the text.
