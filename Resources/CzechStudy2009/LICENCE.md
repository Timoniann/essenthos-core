## What is read, and what NoDerivatives reaches here (RUL-0183)

The verses are served unchanged, every word and sign as the module prints them (the corner brackets ⌈ ⌉ around
wording a note gives literally, the square brackets of supplied words), and no correction is made (owner,
2026-11-24); served for non-commercial use, which this project is (NOT-0014). The links to the Hebrew and the Greek
are a layer beside the text and change none of its words. Loaded beside the verse: the translators' study notes
(26,827) and cross references (34,647). Not read: the section headings the module prints inside the verse
(`<title type="chapter">`), the 22,555 notes pointing to tables of the printed volume (`x-index`, "Pozn. 81 v
tabulce na str. 1499"), and the Strong numbers and parsing codes on 4,377 New Testament words, which tag a word
here and there (`Bůh` with G3588, the article) and are not a tagging of the text.
