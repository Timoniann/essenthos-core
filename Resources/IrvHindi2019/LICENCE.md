## What ShareAlike reaches here (RUL-0183)

ShareAlike binds an **adaptation** of this text and not a collection it stands in. So:

- the text served beside the other texts of the corpus is a collection, and the condition does not
  reach them;
- a **link** row names a Hindi word and a Hebrew or Greek one and holds no text of either — a
  statement about the text, not a version of it. The links loaded for it are Clear Bible's
  (`WLCM-IRVHin-manual`, `SBLGNT-IRVHin-manual`, NLCI), whose own terms are CC BY 4.0;
- the **searchable form** of a Hindi word is an adaptation of this text alone, and carries this
  licence.

## Changes, as the licence asks them to be indicated

The words of the verses are served unchanged. What is not served as text: the book introductions
and outlines (`\ip`, `\is1`, `\io1`, `\iot`), the 2,822 section headings and 5 book headings
(`\s1`, `\ms1`, `\mr`), which are the edition's apparatus. The 2,519 cross references the edition

beside their verses, the brackets removed. None of this is endorsed by Bridge Connectivity
Solutions.
