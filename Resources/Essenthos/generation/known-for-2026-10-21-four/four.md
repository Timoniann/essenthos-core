# «Known for»: Adam, Eve, Jesus and God (FTR-1109), 2026-10-21

**What this is.** The owner asked on 2026-10-21 for the «known for» line for Adam and Eve, and for Jesus and God «якшо не важко». God is the record `yhvh`, the one the site shows as God (LORD / Господь / HERR / Jehová). Made the pilot's way (`../known-for-pilot-2026-10-21/pilot.md`), under the owner's rulings on the pilot: deeds or events a reader would name, more than three where a life earns it but not overdone, no minor details. For Jesus and God: only what the text says, in its own terms; no titles of the risen Christ.

**How it was made.** A first reader (Claude Opus 5.5) read the KJV verses from the live site, read-only, and wrote the clauses with references in English and Ukrainian (`raw/draft.json`; the verses in `raw/packet.txt`). A second, separate reader (another Claude Opus 5.5 run) checked every clause against the KJV text of its verses and the Ukrainian against the English (`raw/check.json`). Its fixes are applied; the first wording is kept in `four.json`. German and Spanish (`renderings-deu-spa.json`) were rendered from the English with the Ukrainian beside it, names as the live site gives them in Luther 1912 and Reina-Valera 1909, and read a second time against the English. No Russian.

**Counts.** 4 records, 16 clauses. Second reading: 13 right, 3 fixed (†).

## What the second reading changed

- **Adam** † — The English is right (GEN 2:7-8, 2:15) and keeps Adam as the implied subject in the passive. The Ukrainian switches to the active «Бог створив його», which makes God the subject of a clause in Adam's own line, unlike his other clauses («дав імена…», «скуштував…»). The passive matches the English and the line. (uk was «Бог створив його з пороху землі й поселив у саду в Едені»)
- **Eve** † — The English is right (GEN 2:21-22). The Ukrainian makes God the subject («Бог створив її») where the line's implied subject is Eve. The passive also carries the feminine agreement the rulings ask for. (uk was «Бог створив її з ребра, взятого в Адама»)
- **Jesus** † — None of the cited verses names Pilate as the one who handed Jesus over. MAT 27:26 says only «he delivered him to be crucified», and Pilate is named as that «he» in MAT 27:24. MRK 15:15 names him in the same sentence: «Pilate… delivered Jesus… to be crucified». (refs was «MAT 27:26, 27:35, 27:50, 27:57-60»)
- **Jesus** — MRK 1:9 added, as the second reading suggested (refs was «MAT 3:13-17»)
- **YHVH** — Ukrainian smoothed, as the second reading suggested (uk was «створив небо й землю, а людину створив на свій образ»)
- **YHVH** — shortened after the check for length; same verses, same sense (en was «brought the Flood upon the earth, then made a covenant with Noah never again to destroy it by a flood»; uk was «навів на землю потоп, а потім уклав із Ноєм заповіт, що потоп більше не знищить її»)
- **YHVH** — EXO 19:20 added, as the second reading suggested (refs was «EXO 12:51, 20:1-17, 31:18»)

## Selection notes not acted on

The second reading's remarks on what else could be chosen. Adam's fatherhood and the Twelve were left out: the current summary already says them («father of Seth, Cain, Abel», «teacher of the Twelve»). Eve's children stay, as the pilot kept Sarah's and Ruth's. The burning bush is already Moses' clause; God's line keeps to five.

- **Adam**: The three clauses cover what a reader names. If a fourth is wanted, the one that is missing is his fatherhood: «fathered Cain, Abel and Seth» (GEN 4:1-2, 4:25, 5:3). That mirrors Eve's clause 3.
- **Eve**: Complete as it stands.
- **Jesus**: The five cover his life from birth to ascension. The clearest candidate for a sixth is «chose twelve apostles» (LUK 6:13, MRK 3:14). It is not required.
- **YHVH**: For the record named YHVH, the one event missing is the giving of the name itself: «called Moses out of the burning bush and told him his name» (EXO 3:4, 3:13-15). Note that in EXO 3:2 it is «the angel of the LORD» who appears in the flame, while God calls from the bush in 3:4, so the actor has to be phrased as in this suggestion. This is the strongest candidate for a sixth clause.
