# Visitors

What simulated visitors are given: `personas.json` holds who they are (each trait a number from 1 to 10 and a sentence saying how it shows on a website; `role` is `visitor` or `expert`, the experts being the data checkers), `goals.json` holds what they come for (`kind` `task` or `explore`, the visitor's own words in `text`, the real Google phrasings behind it in `queries`, the path a search would land on in `landing`, the personas it suits in `fits`, and in `checks` what the session is meant to reveal, which the visitor is never told), `queries.json` is the raw Google autocomplete gathered on 2026-10-14 that the goals were written from, and `visitor-brief.md` is the prompt every visitor agent gets, with one persona and one goal filled into its placeholders. The `visit` tool in essenthos-web reads the persona and goal ids from here; what the sessions record (steps, screenshots, dossiers, reports) lives outside every repository, in `.visitors/` at the workspace root.
