# *.usfm — the Smith–Van Dyck Arabic Bible

**الكتاب المقدس**, the Arabic Bible of the American mission in Beirut: begun by **Eli Smith**
(1801–1857) in 1847 and finished by **Cornelius Van Alen Van Dyck** (1818–1895), the New Testament
in 1860 and the whole Bible in 1865, and printed by the American Bible Society since. It is the
Bible of the Arabic-speaking Protestant churches, printed fully vowelled.

archive taken is `https://ebible.org/Scriptures/arb-vd_usfm.zip` (1,740,174 bytes, last modified
2026-08-08); `scripts/fetch-ebible.ps1 -Only VanDyck1865` is what fetches it, and it re-reads every
statement below before it replaces anything. Downloaded with the owner's approval of 2026-09-25
(NOT-0195, DOC-0207).
